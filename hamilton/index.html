Party values discount the expected gross adjusted box-office revenue (royalties) and the show's free cash flow after the upfront investment (profit shares) at the same rate, with a terminal value at the year-8 growth rate. Producers: 3% royalty plus 42% (45% direct) of free cash flow. Creative team: Miranda, Kail, Blankenbuehler, Lacamoire, Tazewell/Binkley/Steinberg, Chernow and Seller's 5% creative share.
