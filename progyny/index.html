Each curve adds up the discounted cash flows from year 1 through year T and approaches the perpetuity formula value from below. The dots mark where each stream has delivered 50% and 90% of its value. Years to reach a share p: T = ln(1/(1−p)) / ln((1+r)/(1+g)), which does not depend on the first cash flow.

Timing convention: the first cash flow arrives one year from today, so PV = Σ C(1+g)t−1/(1+r)t for t = 1, 2, 3, … The level perpetuity is the special case g = 0.
